## Packets — where failure hides

### T6 Positivity / Overlap Diagnostics (clinical)
- **Established:** Positivity ($P(S=1|X)>0$) and S-admissibility formalized (Degtiar & Rose 2023 An Rev 10.1146/annurev-statistics-042522-103837, Dahabreh 2020 AJE 10.1093/aje/kwy253). Diagnostics toolkit is mature **in IPTW/ATE**: SMD threshold 0.1 (Austin 2009 Stat Med 10.1002/sim.3697), trimming α-cutoffs (Crump 2009 Biometrika), overlap weights $w\propto\min(e,1-e)$ (Li 2018 JASA 10.1080/01621459.2018.1448823).
- **Failure:** Applied transportability studies rarely report these diagnostics (Inoue 2025 Ann Epidemiol 10.1016/j.annepidem.2025.03.001, Kang 2025 Eur J Epidemiol 10.1007/s10654-025-01217-w agree: reporting incomplete; S-admissibility informal, only minority mention trimming). Thresholds borrowed uncalibrated from US/EU treatment propensity — uncharacterized under LMIC shift (Indian: lower BMI/diabetes threshold, younger CVD, selective lab measurement). No study calibrated trimming / SMD on LMIC-shifted covariates or reported transportability overlap plots for an Indian target. Adversarial `India transportability overlap diagnostics` returned zero transportability-specific hits. CARRS (Nair IJE 2022 10.1093/ije/dyac122) and UK Biobank South Asian subset exist as Indian-proxy targets but have not been used with positivity diagnostics.
- **Design delivered:** Graded-shift plasmode using MIMIC-IV/UKB resampling + induced Indian shift, stress-test overlap histograms, SMD before/after weighting, weight summaries (max, ESS, ESS ratio), trimming at α∈{0.01,0.05,0.10} and overlap-weight alternative (Li), plus transversal S-set sensitivity and calibration vs diagnostic threshold mapping (when does standard SMD 0.1 fail to flag calibration collapse?).
- **India verdict:** **STRESSES-ASSUMPTION** — diagnostics' operating characteristics stressed by LMIC covariate/visit-process shift. **Confidence:** Medium (Medium-Low borderline — grey Indian theses may use transportability without keyword).
- **Defeater survival:** Austin/Li/Crump alone do not defeat (treatment positivity ≠ transport positivity); Inoue/Kang are descriptive, not evaluative; PLOS ONE 2022 single-US example not systematic. A systematic LMIC diagnostics study would collapse the gap.

### T4 Prescribing Audit → Anchored E-value + Negative Controls (clinical)
- **Established:** E-value minimal-assumption sensitivity (VanderWeele & Ding 2017 Ann Int Med 10.7326/M16-2607, 3000+ cites) is consensus; quantitative bias analysis present in <15% of EHR studies (Zhang BMJ Medicine 2023 10.1136/bmjmed-2022-000366; J Clin Epidemiol 2023 10.1016/j.jclinepi.2023.09.014). Target-trial emulation framework normative (Hernán 2024 Ann Int Med 10.7326/ANNALS-24-01871, Hernán 2025 JAMA 10.1001/jamanetworkopen.2025.58262). Negative controls canonical (Lipsitch 2010 Epidemiology 10.1097/EDE.0b013e3181d61eeb) and regulatory-expected (Duke/FDA Workshop 2023) but usage <10-20% in published EHR.
- **Failure:** E-values reported generically (e.g., 1.5) **without anchoring** to plausible confounder magnitudes. Indian prescribing audits using WHO core indicators are abundant and consistently quantify precisely the needed anchors — polypharmacy, irrational FDCs, 30-50% generic/EDL non-compliance, antibiotic overuse 40-60%, cost-driven substitution (Global Heart polypill survey 10.5334/gh.1335 PMC11225556), concomitant Ayurveda/AYUSH co-use 10-40% in chronic disease (survey 10.4103/ayu.ayu_81_20, unmeasured in EHR, treatment-correlated) — but these audits **do not cite causal sensitivity literature**, and sensitivity papers **do not cite audits**. No identified study translates audit prevalence into VanderWeele E-value parameters or benchmarks negative-control false-positive rate on Indian routine care (time-zero ambiguity, out-of-system purchase, fractional dosing). Adversarial `India target trial emulation negative control` returned zero. OHDSI LEGEND (US/EU/Korea/Japan) has no Indian OMOP site identified; no Indian emulation with negative controls located.
- **Design delivered:** Same target trial (ACEi/ARB vs CCB or metformin vs sulfonylurea) emulated in (a) US benchmark (MIMIC-IV/OMOP) and (b) Indian-typical plasmode (cost-driven switching + MNAR labs + AYUSH latent confounder with audit-derived injection parameters) or real Indian-proxy (CARRS/UKB-SA), reporting PS/overlap diagnostics + E-value vs audit-anchored threshold + ≥1 negative-control outcome (fracture) + ≥1 negative-control exposure, comparing whether anchored thresholds overturn recommendation and whether controls correctly null.
- **India verdict:** **STRESSES-ASSUMPTION** — exchangeability, positivity, consistency, time-zero all stressed by Indian care patterns; audit→E-value translation is the India-specific methodological contribution. **Confidence:** Medium (borderline Medium-High — OHDSI India network or 2024-25 preprint could exist under different terminology).
- **Defeater survival:** Zhang/Hernán/IP papers are generic, not audit-anchored; LEGEND is near-miss but geographically disjoint; characterizing AYUSH prevalence ≠ building it into bias analysis.

### T1 Plasmode Design (methods)
- **Established:** Sun 2026 Health Data Sci 10.34133/hds.0456 review (DL-for-ISMTS architecture-saturated, benchmark-poor); joint longitudinal–survival mature (JMbayes2/joineRML, Rizopoulos); informative visiting decomposable (Liang arXiv 2410.13113 three-process: visit + observation + longitudinal with shared Gaussian frailty $b_i$); simulation guidance for joint vs Cox (Schneider 2025 BioData Mining 10.1186/s13040-025-00450-z PMC12070788 varying frequency/noise/heterogeneity); plasmode foundations (Franklin Am J Epidemiol 10.1093/aje/kww098, Schuler); mandatory DL baselines GRU-D (Che Sci Rep 10.1038/s41598-018-24271-9), SeFT (Horn ICML 2020), plus JMbayes2.
- **Failure:** No published study jointly varies **visit informativeness ($\gamma_v$) + observation informativeness ($\gamma_o$/δ) + sparsity ($\lambda_V$) + noise ($\sigma$/SNR)** with **known truth** and compares **DL irregular-series suite vs classical LMM/joint** on **calibration/coverage/DCA**, not only AUC. Naemi 2024 MIMIC-IV tabular DL benchmark (arXiv 2401.15290) is DL-vs-DL, classical-poor; Schneider is joint-vs-Cox only; Liang is within joint-model family (no GRU-D/SeFT). Plasmode Generate-Treatment vs Generate-Outcome choice (Liu arXiv 2504.11740 cautionary) reverses conclusions if misspecified — untested for this task.
- **Design delivered (executable, pre-register-able):** Generative spec §7a (3-process joint: $\lambda_{V,i}(t)=\lambda_{0V}(t)\exp(\gamma_v b_i +\beta_v^T X_i + \alpha_v Y^*(t-))$, $\text{logit }P(O_{ij}=1|{\rm visit})=\gamma_o b_i + \beta_o^T X_i + \delta Y^*$, $Y_{ij}=X\beta+Z b_i+\epsilon$, shared $b_i$, outcome $\text{logit }P(E)= \theta_0+\theta_1 f(Y^*)+\theta_2 b_i$); parameter inventory for pre-register: N {500,2k,10k}, visits/patient 2/6/15, horizon 3/5y, SNR 0.5/1.5/4, $\gamma_v$ 0/0.3/0.8, $\gamma_o$ 0/0.4/0.9, heterogeneity $\tau_0$ 0.5/1.5 $\tau_1$ 0.2/0.8, effect 1.1/1.5/2.5 HR; core 16-cell fractional design ×200 MC replicates + sensitivity sweeps; mandatory baselines LMM ($lme4$/$nlme$) + JMbayes2 + LOCF+logistic + MICE + GRU-D + SeFT (+ optional GRU-ODE via torchdiffeq); metrics AUC/C-index + calibration slope/intercept (Riley/Van Calster) + Brier/I-Brier + 90/95% prediction-interval coverage + DCA net benefit; twin plasmode variants; **primary decision rule:** DL wins only if non-inferior on calibration (|slope-1|≤0.1, intercept ≤0.1 logit) and coverage (within 2 pp) *and* superior on DCA at clinical thresholds.
- **India verdict:** GEOGRAPHY-ONLY for v1; Stage-2 Indian-regime sparsity extension genuine but separate. **Confidence:** Medium — Sun supplement/code (github.com/SCXsunchenxi/ISMTS-Review) + Naemi follow-ups must be inspected before promotion.
- **Defeater survival:** Naemi is narrow (no classical); Schneider narrow (no DL); Sun is review, not experiment. Any extension adding the exact conjunction ($γ_v$/γ_o decomposition + LMM/joint + GRU-D/SeFT + calibration/coverage/DCA) would close the gap.
