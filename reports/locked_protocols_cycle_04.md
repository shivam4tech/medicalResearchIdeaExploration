### T1 Plasmode Lock (methods) — GEOGRAPHY-ONLY v1 · Medium
- **Established:** 3-process joint generative family (Liang arXiv 2410.13113 visit+observation+longitudinal with shared Gaussian frailty b_i); ISMTS review Sun 2026 `10.34133/hds.0456`; joint-model guidance Schneider 2025 BioData Mining `10.1186/s13040-025-00450-z`; plasmode foundations Franklin 2016 `10.1093/aje/kww098` + Schuler; baselines GRU-D `10.1038/s41598-018-24271-9`, SeFT (Horn ICML 2020), JMbayes2/joineRML (Rizopoulos).
- **Failure:** No study jointly varies γ_v (visit informativeness) + γ_o (observation informativeness) + λ_V (sparsity) + σ (noise/SNR) with known truth and pits DL irregular-series suite vs classical LMM/joint on **calibration/coverage/DCA** (not AUC only). Naemi 2024 MIMIC-IV tabular DL benchmark is DL-vs-DL; Schneider is joint-vs-Cox; Liang is within-joint; Generate-Treatment vs Generate-Outcome fragility (Liu 2504.11740) untested for this comparison.
- **Locked protocol delivered (§7, code-pointer level):**
  - **Generative spec restated (copy-paste for OSF):** λ_V,i(t)=λ_0V·exp(γ_v·b_i + β_v^T X_i + α_v·Y*(t-)), logit P(O_ij=1|visit)=γ_o·b_i + β_o^T X_i + δ·Y*_ij, Y_ij = X_ij β + Z_ij b_i + ε_ij, ε~N(0,σ²), b_i~N(0,Σ_τ), outcome logit P(E_i=1)=θ_0 + θ_1·f(Y*_history) + θ_2·b_i — shared b_i induces visit/observation informativeness. λ_0V Weibull or constant baseline.
  - **Twin plasmode variants:** Generate-Treatment (exposure from Y*) vs Generate-Outcome (Y* from exposure) — both pre-registered; any method that inverts under variant swap is flagged as plasmode-fragile (primary robustness test).
  - **16-cell core grid (pre-registered, fractional) ×200 MC:** N {500, 2k, 10k} × visits/patient {2,6,15} × SNR {0.5,1.5,4} × γ_v {0,0.3,0.8} × γ_o {0,0.4,0.9} × τ_1 {0.2,0.8} — 16 cells cover corners+center via Latin hypercube (packet Table 7b); sensitivity sweeps fill remaining factorial post-core.
  - **Code pointers (no guessing):** `lme4`/`nlme` (LMM), `JMbayes2`/`joineRML` (joint longitudinal-survival), LOCF+logistic, `mice` (MICE), `torch` GRU-D (Che implementation `mlds-lab/grud`), SeFT (Horn `ratschlab/seft`), GRU-ODE via `torchdiffeq` optional — all pinned versions in `renv`/`requirements.txt`.
  - **Metrics:** AUC/C-index + calibration slope/intercept (Van Calster) + flexible calibration plot + Brier/I-Brier + 90/95% prediction interval coverage + DCA net benefit at 10%/20%/30% thresholds (Vickers).
  - **Compute estimate (single-GPU, packet §7m):** ~16 cells ×200 reps ×7 baselines ≈ 22,400 fits; per-fit 2–30 min (LMM/fast vs JMbayes2/GRU-D 20–30 min) → ~200–300 GPU-hours on A100-equivalent, checkpointed every 10 reps, parallelizable to 2–3 days on 4 GPUs; planner table provided for down-scaling (96-cell →16-cell justification).
  - **Decision rule:** DL wins only if non-inferior on calibration (|slope-1|≤0.1, |intercept|≤0.1 logit) AND coverage within 2 pp of nominal AND superior on DCA at ≥2 thresholds — otherwise classical suffices (publishable negative).
- **India verdict:** GEOGRAPHY-ONLY v1; Indian-regime sparsity arm (λ_V low + selective observation) staged as extension.
- **Defeater survival:** Review/background (Sun, Schneider) ≠ head-to-head DL-vs-classical on calibration/coverage/DCA across γ_v/γ_o decomposition.
