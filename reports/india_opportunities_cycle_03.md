### T4 Audit→RR Translation (clinical) — STRESSES-ASSUMPTION · Medium
- **Established:** VanderWeele E-value 10.7326/M16-2607 (E=RR+√(RR(RR-1))) canonical, 3000+ cites; bias-factor refinement B(p1,p0,RR_UD)=[p1(RR_UD-1)+1]/[p0(RR_UD-1)+1], B_max=RR_EU×RR_UD/(RR_EU+RR_UD-1). Zhang BMJ Medicine 2023 10.1136/bmjmed-2022-000366 (QBA <15% reporting, rarely anchored), J Clin Epidemiol 2023 10.1016/j.jclinepi.2023.09.014 (use/misinterpretation), Lipsitch 2010 Epidemiology 10.1097/EDE.0b013e3181d61eeb (negative controls), Hernán 2024 Ann Int Med 10.7326/ANNALS-24-01871 + Duke/FDA Workshop 2023 (NC expected but <10–20% used).
- **Failure demonstrated:** The WHO-indicator audit corpus and causal sensitivity corpus **do not cite each other** — every audit (Kaur/Khanna) inspected has zero VanderWeele citations; every E-value paper has zero WHO audit citations. Adversarial sweep `audit to E-value bridge` returned E-value calculators/tutorials/methods (J Clin Epidemiol, Aqrab guide, MetricGate, bias-amplification sim, medRxiv hybrid) that compute E-values generically, never from audit prevalences. **MUST extracts satisfied:** Kaur 2026 Cureus 10.7759/cureus.109912 (PMC13312064, Tables 1–10: n=648, 2.65 drugs/Rx, generic 64.9%, NLEM 87.3%, injections 90.3%, diagnosis 8.5%, night injection OR 2.78) + Khanna 2025 Cureus 10.7759/cureus.99580 (PMC12813935, Tables 2–6: n=300, 6.8 drugs/Rx, generic 4.7%, NLEM 61%, polypharmacy 71%, irrational FDC market 79.5%) provide the full translation inputs; Galib 2020 AYU 10.4103/ayu.ayu_81_20 (95.9% herbo-mineral concomitant, 45.3% under supervision, ~44% simultaneous) gives AYUSH P(U) 10–40% (national) →44–96% (enriched).
- **Design delivered:** Audit→RR translation bridge: extract audit marginals → impute conditional p1=P(U|E=1), p0=P(U|E=0) for the specific contrast (irrational-FDC vs single-agent: p1 15–25% vs p0 2% → RR_EU 7.5–12; AYUSH-plus-allopathy vs allopathy: p1 44–96% vs p0 10% → RR_EU 4.4–9.6) → anchor RR_UD from outcome literature (herb-induced liver injury 1.5–3.0, irrational FDC ADR 1.3–1.8) or sweep → compute B and fixed-point **R* where E-value(R*)=B** — the audit-anchored decision threshold per contrast (e.g., RR_obs 1.45 → E=2.26 vs B 1.31 at AYUSH p1=0.44 RR_UD=2.0 → robust; R*≈1.4–2.0 across titration). Titration contour over two-audit envelope + RR_UD 1.2→4.0; NC falsification ladder alongside (Lipsitch; trauma/trauma-adjacent for antihypertensive comparison; OHDSI LEGEND panel analogue). Plasmode deployment: MIMIC-IV plasmode at G1–G3 P(U) 0.10/0.44/0.96, measure false-"robust" rate under audit-plausible prevalence.
- **India verdict:** **STRESSES-ASSUMPTION** — AYUSH EHR-invisibility (exchangeability), irrational FDC treatment-version (consistency), polypharmacy positivity (71% polypharmacy → mono vs combo non-overlap), missing diagnosis 91.5% (informative missingness/time-zero) all quantified by audit numbers and mapped to bias factor. **Confidence:** Medium (two audits with tables + full causal chain 302-verified, but p1-p0 imputation is translation assumption — audits report prescription-level not arm-level P(U|E) — RR_UD from adjacent ADR literature not Indian outcome-linked, Khanna internal inconsistency 23.1% vs 45.7% antibiotics / 70% vs 29% diagnosis).
- **Defeater survival:** J Clin Epidemiol tutorials enumerate E-value computation but never substitute audit P(U); Zhang reports absence of anchoring (supports gap); Duke NC perspective is falsification not anchoring.

### T2 Ahlqvist HTE Transport (methods) — STRESSES-ASSUMPTION · Medium
- **Established:** Ahlqvist Lancet Diabetes 2018 10.1016/s2213-8587(18)30051-2 (n=8980 ANDIS, 5 clusters SAID/SIDD/SIRD/MOD/MARD from GADA/age/BMI/HbA1c/HOMA2-B/HOMA2-IR, 3 Scandinavian replications, 2086 cites), East Asian replications show systematic proportion shift (SIRD under-represented, SIDD/MOD enriched at lower BMI), Indian descriptive clustering exists (Anjana BMJ Open Diabetes 10.1136/bmjdrc-2020-001506) but typically omits GADA/HOMA and reports no formal transport test. Transportability formalism (Pearl & Bareinboim 2014 Stat Sci 10.1214/14-STS486, Degtiar & Rose 2023 Ann Rev, Dahabreh 2020 AJE 10.1093/aje/kwy253, Kang 2025 scoping 10.1007/s10654-025-01217-w, Levy 2024 J Comp Eff Res 10.57264/cer-2024-0064 N=6 all US/Canada). HTE methods (Wager & Athey 2018 JASA 10.1080/01621459.2017.1319839 causal forests, Künzel PNAS 10.1073/pnas.1804597116 metalearners, Secher 2016 Pharm Stat).
- **Failure demonstrated:** Existing Indian Ahlqvist replications are **de novo clustering**, not a **falsifiable transport test** (apply Ahlqvist centroids vs re-discover de novo with same k-means spec, with positivity/overlap diagnostics and outcome-gradient replication). Adversarial sweeps `Ahlqvist Indian replication ICMR INDIAB CARRS` + `diabetes clusters India 5 subtypes` returned descriptive clusters, no paper with pre-registered centroids, inverse-odds weighting, S-admissibility, and TRIPOD+AI-style reporting. GADA autoantibody and HOMA2 (fasting C-peptide/insulin) systematically unavailable in Indian primary care / many tertiary settings — a measurement-transport asymmetry no study formalizes.
- **Design delivered:** Falsifiable empirical transport: source ANDIS published centroids/SDs (no individual data needed) vs targets CARRS (Delhi/Chennai/Karachi n~12k) / ICMR-INDIAB (n~113k state-stratified) / CMC-AIIMS T2D registry (richer GADA/HOMA) — and UKB-SA n~8k proxy if access delayed. Arms: transport-labels (standardize on ANDIS means/SDs, nearest centroid, assignment completeness % within 2 SD + silhouette + proportion χ² vs ANDIS) vs de novo (k=5 k-means on Indian data, adjusted Rand index + outcome-gradient concordance) with ablatives: 6-var ideal vs 4-var (proxy) vs 3-var (GADA-free). Positivity diagnostics (inverse-odds weighting Dahabreh, propensity Scandinavian vs Indian distribution, overlap coefficient, ESS, truncation 1/5/10%, SMD per S-variable). Outcomes: CKD/eGFR decline + retinopathy + insulin initiation (HRs vs MARD reference, KM). Mandatory baselines: de novo vs random, k-means vs GMM/hierarchical, GADA-free sensitivity, continuous risk model (Kent) comparison. Measurement stress reported explicitly (6→3 var verdict change is the finding).
- **India verdict:** **STRESSES-ASSUMPTION** — exchangeability of clustering features (GADA/age/BMI/HbA1c/HOMA2 distributions differ by ancestry/environment), positivity (young/low-BMI tail has no Scandinavian counterpart), S-admissibility (health-system measurement process S-node). Not GEOGRAPHY-ONLY — the question is whether heterogeneity itself transports under positivity when measurement differs. **Confidence:** Medium (transport formalism + Ahlqvist landscape clearly surveyed, but Indian cluster supplements must be PDF-extracted for hidden overlap analysis, CARRS/ICMR-INDIAB phenotyping depth for GADA/HOMA unconfirmed without DUA, sampling-frame comparability ANDIS new-onset vs CARRS population).
- **Defeater survival:** Existing Indian cluster papers lack weighting/S-admissibility; East Asian shift shows plausibility not methods novelty; formal transport estimators exist in principle but Levy 2024 shows almost zero LMIC application.
