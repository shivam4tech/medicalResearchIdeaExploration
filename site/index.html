Clinical–Computational Methods Research Programme

Seven pre-registered studies asking one plain question: which Western methods still work when the patients, labs, and clinic routines look different — especially in India? All designs, pilots and full 40k/8k synthetic runs are frozen and logged. Real hospital data is the next staged step, not a blocker.

Glossary

Glossary — plain English for doctors

Every abbreviation on this page, alphabetical, one sentence each — ward language first, methods label underneath. Hover tooltips on cards link here.

A

AmsterdamUMCdb · ICU database
23,000 de-identified ICU admissions from Amsterdam University Medical Center, used as an external hospital to test whether a model still works.
Open critical-care dataset — Europe.
ANDIS · All New Diabetics in Scania
The Swedish study that first discovered the five diabetes clusters (SAID/SIDD/SIRD/MOD/MARD) we re-test in South Asians.
Ahlqvist et al. Lancet Diabetes Endocrinol 2018.
ARI · Adjusted Rand Index
A score from 0 (random) to 1 (perfect) for whether two ways of grouping patients actually agree; 0.25 on our SA proxy means labels exist but meaning has shifted.
Threshold ≥0.60 to claim clusters transport.

B

B · calibration intercept (bias)
Whether the model systematically predicts too high or too low across all patients; 0 is perfect, positive means over-estimation.
Paired with slope — both needed per Van Calster.
BMI · Body Mass Index
Weight divided by height squared; South Asians develop diabetes at lower BMI (thin-fat), so European BMI cut-offs mislead.
G0 28.3 → G3 22.8 in our tilting.

C

CARRS · Centre for Cardiometabolic Risk Reduction in South Asia
12,000 adults followed in Delhi, Chennai and Karachi since 2010 — our primary South Asian cohort for validating clusters and tilting.
Emory + PHFI steering — DUA 2–3 months.
CIMEHR · simulation engine
Software that simulates the “sicker patients get measured more often” problem so we can benchmark prediction methods without waiting for new data.
v0.1.0 — 16-cell visit-informativeness sweep.

D

DCA · Decision Curve Analysis
A way to judge whether using the model to decide who to treat actually helps patients at a chosen risk threshold like 10% or 20%.
Net benefit — co-primary in 002.
DUA · Data Use Agreement
The legal contract you sign before a hospital lets you analyse its de-identified patient data; our staged access is 1–6 months.
No PHI leaves the source — governance-gated.

E

eICU · eICU Collaborative Research Database
139,000 ICU stays from 208 US hospitals — a very different setting from the single Boston hospital the original model was trained on.
Philips / MIT — external validation target.
E-value · sensitivity to hidden bias
The minimum strength an unmeasured factor must have to fully explain away an observed effect; larger means more robust.
Kin to R* — we report both.
ESS · Effective Sample Size
After re-weighting US data to look like Indian data, how many patients’ worth of information is still usable; ESS/n = 0.005 means only 0.5% remains.
ESS/n <0.02 flags “must re-tune” at G2.

G

GADA · Glutamic Acid Decarboxylase Antibodies
A blood test for autoimmune diabetes; positive suggests type 1–like disease, but it is rarely measured in Indian clinics (<10% in CARRS).
Simulated Bernoulli 5.5% pending dictionary.
GRU-D · Gated Recurrent Unit with Decay
A deep-learning model that handles irregular lab timings by learning how long since the last measurement; tested against classical joint models.
Benchmarked in CIMEHR 003.

H

HbA1c · glycated haemoglobin
Average blood sugar over ~3 months; used as one of the three routine tests that might replace the six-test cluster version.
Included in 3-var ablation.
HOMA2-B · beta-cell function
An estimate of how much insulin your pancreas still makes, calculated from fasting glucose and insulin/C-peptide.
Needed for SIDD/SIRD — rarely available.
HOMA2-IR · insulin resistance
An estimate of how resistant your body is to its own insulin; high values drive the SIRD cluster.
Paired with HOMA2-B.

I

ICI · Integrated Calibration Index
The average gap between predicted risk and observed risk across all patients; 0 is perfect, above 0.02 flags meaningful miscalibration.
Van Calster hierarchy — with slope.
ICMR-INDIAB · Indian Council of Medical Research – India Diabetes Study
113,043 adults sampled across all 31 Indian states (2008–2020) — the national reference for how diabetes actually looks in India.
MONO 43.3% national, 34.8–56.7% state range.

M

MARD · Mild Age-Related Diabetes
The “older, mild” Swedish diabetes subgroup — later onset, modest metabolic disturbance.
One of ANDIS five — least severe.
MIMIC-III · Medical Information Mart for Intensive Care III
De-identified ICU records from one Boston hospital — where the original 2019 LSTM was trained.
Source domain for 001 and 002.
MIMIC-IV · MIMIC version 4
The updated Boston ICU dataset (more recent years, more patients) used as the “real” target when testing the synthetic ladder.
Target in fidelity ladder 002.
MOD · Mild Obesity-Related Diabetes
The “younger, heavier” Swedish diabetes subgroup — obesity-driven but not severely insulin-resistant.
ANDIS — BMI-driven.
MONO · Metabolically Obese Normal Weight (thin-fat)
People with normal BMI but high body fat and metabolic risk — common in South Asians (56.7% rural Tripura vs 0% US-like G0).
Key tilting axis G0→G3.

O

OSF · Open Science Framework
A public timestamping service where we froze every study design before seeing outcomes, so we cannot move the goalposts later.
All OSF files shown in tables are timestamped.

P

PROBAST · Prediction model Risk Of Bias Assessment Tool
A checklist for judging whether a prediction-model study is at high risk of bias in participants, predictors, outcome or analysis.
Used alongside TRIPOD.

R

R* · robustness value
How strong an unmeasured confounder (e.g. hidden AYUSH use) must be to shrink an observed risk ratio to 1; 1.0 is fragile, 1.5 is robust.
Our 9-cell matrix: 1.001–1.531.
RECORD · REporting of studies Conducted using Observational Routinely-collected Data
An extension of STROBE that adds what you must report when using routine health records instead of purpose-collected data.
For EHR-based studies.
RR · Registered Report
A journal format where peer review happens on the study design before results exist, so publication cannot depend on finding something exciting.
Stage-1 RR lines counted — 1,624 total.

S

SAID · Severe Autoimmune Diabetes
The autoimmune diabetes subgroup (GADA-positive) — closest to type 1, needs insulin early.
ANDIS — rare in South Asians pending check.
SIDD · Severe Insulin-Deficient Diabetes
The “pancreas failing” subgroup — low insulin production, high HbA1c, high complication risk.
ANDIS — HOMA2-B low.
SIRD · Severe Insulin-Resistant Diabetes
The “very resistant” subgroup — high insulin needs, strong link to kidney disease.
ANDIS — HOMA2-IR high.
SMD · Standardised Mean Difference
How far apart two groups’ averages are in standard-deviation units; above 0.1 is often taken as meaningful imbalance.
Balance diagnostic for transport.
STROBE · Strengthening the Reporting of Observational studies in Epidemiology
The standard checklist for reporting observational studies so readers can judge what was actually done.
RECORD extends STROBE for EHR.
synthEHRella · synthetic EHR generator
Software that creates artificial patient records at graded levels of realism to find the lowest fidelity where method rankings still hold.
74aa516 — S1–S5 ladder in 002.

T

TRIPOD · Transparent Reporting of a multivariable prediction model for Individual Prognosis Or Diagnosis
The 22-item checklist for reporting prediction-model studies completely and reproducibly.
Basis for 004 corpus audit.
TRIPOD+AI · TRIPOD for Artificial Intelligence
The 2024 update (27 items, Collins et al. BMJ) that adds AI-specific reporting such as data leakage and fairness checks.
27-item + 6-item leakage checklist.

U

UKB-SA · UK Biobank South Asian
About 8,000 British South Asian adults within the UK Biobank — a managed-access proxy while Indian DUAs are pending.
Field 21001 — staged 1–3 months.

W

Wilson CI · Wilson confidence interval
A confidence interval for a proportion (e.g. “31% of papers”) that stays honest even when the proportion is small; we use Riley’s 95% method.
Primary 0.313 [0.245, 0.391] in 004.
