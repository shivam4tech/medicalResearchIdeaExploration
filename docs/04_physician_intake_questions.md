# 04 — Physician Intake Questions (deferred, non-blocking)

*To be answered by the physician collaborator when convened. Exploration proceeds on
public knowledge meanwhile. Ordered by how much each answer constrains project choice.*

## A. Clinical domain & interests
1. Specialty / subspecialty exposure? Clinical areas of strongest personal interest?
2. Clinical questions you keep encountering where the evidence feels weak or contradictory?
3. Types of decisions in your practice where prognostic uncertainty dominates?

## B. Patients & populations
4. Typical patient population (age structure, multimorbidity, socioeconomic mix)?
5. Diseases/conditions where you suspect Indian patients genuinely differ from published
   (mostly Western) cohorts — in presentation, progression, or treatment response?
6. Where do you see treatment-response variability that guidelines ignore?

## C. Data reality
7. What data does your institution routinely capture? (diagnoses, labs, prescribing,
   imaging reports, outcomes) — and how structured is it?
8. Typical measurement frequency and follow-up losses — how irregular is the record?
9. What is documented reliably vs. notoriously missing or free-text?
10. Roughly what sample sizes could realistically be assembled? Over what time horizon?
11. Are outcomes (death, readmission, response) linkable/recorded, or are they lost?

## D. Practice patterns that might break imported methods
12. Prescribing patterns that differ from international guidelines (drugs, dosing,
    thresholds, timing)?
13. Diagnostic or triage pathways that differ (e.g., late presentation, different
    referral chains)?
14. Resource-driven practice: tests ordered only when indicated → informative missingness?

## E. Governance
15. Institutional ethics committee? Typical approval pathway and timeline?
16. Any prior experience with data-sharing for research? Consent models in use?
17. Hard constraints: what data would *never* leave the institution?

## F. Judgment calls for candidate review
18. For each shortlisted candidate later: "If this question were answered well, would it
    change anything you do, teach, or believe?"
